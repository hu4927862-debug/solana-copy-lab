Read docs/PROJECT_STATE.md and docs/DECISIONS.md first. Check Git status and
preserve unrelated changes. Use the offline demo and focused tests.

identity, accounting, responsibility or safety checks to bypass omitted private

paper results are not finalized realized net returns.
