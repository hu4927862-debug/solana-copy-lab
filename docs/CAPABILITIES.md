# Capability and evidence map

This map describes the public source and its offline tests. It is not a current
mainnet qualification, external security audit, or profitability claim. The
supported entry points are documented in [USAGE.md](USAGE.md).

**Evidence levels:** `OFFLINE_TESTED` means a fixture or mock exercises the
linked behavior. `CODE_PRESENT` means the source is available for inspection.
Neither means `LIVE_QUALIFIED`. The minimized historical fixtures are reconstructed
regression representations; the four provider-neutral JSON inputs also lack the
complete published RPC capture chain. Neither is a fresh account/program attestation.
See [fixture provenance](../test/fixtures/README.md).

## What is not claimed

- No independent external audit certification, universal token safety, automatic
  scam detection, absolute anti-MEV guarantee, or production availability SLA.
- No live trading quickstart, present program qualification or funds authority.
- No proven follower net expectancy, model ranking or profitable-wallet list.
- No full support for every Jupiter instruction, Token-2022 extension or DEX.

## Research value without a live quickstart

The public snapshot deliberately supplies no qualified funded quickstart. It
still retains historical signing, submission and responsibility source for
inspection; publication did not remove those modules or grant their authority.
Validity, freshness, route and risk filters answer whether an observation fits
their contract. They do not establish a predictive alpha signal.

Quote and paper examples leave real fill, network/tip and failed execution costs
unresolved. This evidence does not prove that copying wallets is universally
negative expectancy, always buys the top or incurs a fixed percentage of
slippage. A future predictive claim would need a separately frozen signal,
comparison benchmark and new prospective evidence; this release makes none.
MEV and execution limits remain in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md), without an
immunity guarantee.

Supported offline checks can demonstrate boundaries and reproducibility without
turning these unknowns into successful trading claims. Keep classification,
quote availability, execution scope, qualification and permission separate.
